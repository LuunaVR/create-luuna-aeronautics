# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-24

This Privacy Policy explains how the Luuna Aeronautics Discord Rich Presence integration (the "App") handles data.

## 1. Data we process

The App may process limited gameplay status information needed to display Discord Rich Presence, such as:

- Current game activity/state text
- Basic session status needed for presence updates

## 2. Data we do not collect for sale

The App is not intended to sell personal data.

## 3. Storage and sharing

- The App is designed to minimize data handling and only use what is required for Rich Presence operation.
- Data is shared with Discord only as required to show your Rich Presence status.
- The App maintainers do not intentionally share your data with unrelated third parties.

## 4. Retention

The App is intended to avoid retaining personal data beyond what is necessary to provide Rich Presence functionality.

## 5. Your choices

- You can disable or stop using the App at any time.
- You can control Discord visibility settings through your Discord account.

## 6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates are effective when posted on this page.
